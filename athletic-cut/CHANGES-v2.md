# Athletic Cut v2: what changed

This is the `athletic-cut-v2` branch. It is not merged into main. Nothing changes on your phone until you approve the merge.

Your data is safe. v2 reads the same storage as v1, and a test proves it: a backup made by v1, and a phone that already has v1 data, both open in v2 with every session, weigh-in, scan and setting intact.

## Try this on your phone

1. **Open Home.** You'll see today's session as numbered steps, a strip with days A–D plus Optional (done, today and next), bigger stat tiles and a weekly ring.
2. **Start a session and finish the prep.** The bar at the top now names the phases: Prep, Lift, Superset, Finish, Mobility.
3. **On the main lift**, look under the load. It shows the plates for each side. Set your bar and trap-bar weight, and tick the plates you own, in **More → Workout → Plate calculator**.
4. **Open "Warm-up (optional)"** and tap a row. It logs a warm-up set without starting the rest timer.
5. **Tap "History"** under Last to see your best set and your last three sessions.
6. **Log a set.** You get a quick "Set 1 saved", then REST with the next set right under the timer. The sets you've done are listed below the inputs.
7. **Tap ✕ at the top left.** You'll get **Pause and leave**, **Finish early** (saves what you did) and **Discard** (asks first).
8. **Finish a session.** You'll see duration, sets done against planned, volume, a green card for your best record, and the next day.
9. **Go to More → History**, open a session and tap a set to correct it. You can also edit the note.
10. **Go to Library.** Tap ★ on an exercise, then try the Favorites and Recent filters. Each demo has Start, Finish and Play buttons.
11. **Go to Progress.** It opens with one line that sums up the range.
12. **The exercise pictures are now line drawings** (all 38), in the library, the lift-screen thumbnails and the demo. Start and Finish share one floor line so the toggle doesn't jump. They follow light and dark mode. Custom exercises you add still get the stick figure.

## Not done in this round

- **Exercise drawings (Phase B, item 9).** The stick figures are unchanged. Redrawing all 38 in one consistent style needs its own pass, and the limb-length test should stay as it is.
- **Tappable charts on Progress.** The takeaway headline and sticky range controls are in, but tapping a chart point doesn't show a value yet.

## Questions for you (Phase C, not built)

1. **12 vs 14 weeks.** The program data says 12 weeks, "up to 14". The app counts "Week N of 12". What should happen after week 12? Keep counting (week 13 of 12)? Add up to two extra weeks? Or end the program?
2. **Deloads.** The program data has no deload weeks. Do you want planned lighter weeks? If so, when, and how much lighter?
3. **Substitutions and equipment.** Only the main lifts have program-approved swaps. Do you want an equipment profile ("no trap bar here today") that picks swaps automatically, with separate histories for each swap?
4. **Rescheduling.** Days run in order and a missed day just waits. Do you want a calendar where you can move a day?
5. **CSV export.** Is JSON plus the weekly Markdown report enough, or do you want spreadsheets?
6. **Share card.** v1 deliberately has no social sharing. Should v2 make an image of a finished session, made on the phone? It's your call.
7. **Apple Health / Health Connect.** This isn't possible in a web app that runs as a single file. It would need a native app.
